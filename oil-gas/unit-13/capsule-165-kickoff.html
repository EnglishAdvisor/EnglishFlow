Activity 1 — Petroleum Products from Crude Oil

Crude oil is refined into 6 main petroleum products. Study each product — its physical state, boiling point range, and typical uses.

Petroleum Gas
LPG / liquid petroleum gas
Gas
Cooking, heating, fuel for vehicles. Stored as liquid under pressure.
Petrol
gasoline (AmE)
Liquid
Fuel for cars and light vehicles.
Kerosene
jet fuel / paraffin
Liquid
Jet aircraft fuel, heating, cooking.
Diesel
petrodiesel
Liquid
Trucks, buses, trains, ships, generators.
Fuel Oil
heavy oil / bunker fuel
Liquid
Ship engines, power stations, industrial heating.
Asphalt
bitumen
Solid
Road surfaces, roofing, waterproofing.
