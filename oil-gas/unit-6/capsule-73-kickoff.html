Drilling — quick vocab
derrick — tall steel structure on rig
drill bit — cuts / crushes rock at the bottom
drill string — long string of connected pipes
rotary table — rotates the drill string
mud pump — pushes drilling mud down
mud tank — holds the drilling mud
mud screen — separates cuttings from mud
cuttings — rock pieces from drilling
pipe rack — where spare pipes are stored
